
#### 1. Product Name
- Working Title: Movie App

#### 2. Vision Statement

- To help users discover, explore, and confidently decide what to watch through a fast, intuitive, and well-structured movie discovery experience.

#### 3. Mission Statement
- My mission is to reduce the effort users spend searching across multiple sources by bringing essential movie information into one clean, intuitive, and reliable experience.
Instead of overwhelming users with information, the application will present only what helps them make confident viewing decisions.

#### 4. Problem Statement
- Many users spend several minutes deciding what to watch.
They often switch between multiple applications or websites to gather information such as: Ratings, Story overview, Cast, Trailers, Similar movies, Release details.

This fragmented experience increases the time and effort required to make a viewing decision.
This application aims to simplify this process by presenting relevant information in a structured and easy-to-consume manner.

#### 5. Target Audience
- Primary Users:

People who want to quickly discover movies and decide what to watch without spending excessive time searching across different sources.
Examples:
Weekend movie watchers
Casual viewers
Working professionals looking for entertainment after work
- Secondary Users

Movie enthusiasts who enjoy exploring detailed information such as: Cast, Genres, Ratings, Similar movies, Collections
Behind-the-scenes details (future scope)

#### 6. Product Goals
The application should enable users to:
- Discover trending and popular movies.
- Search for movies quickly.
- Explore complete movie details.
- Understand whether a movie matches their interests.
- Navigate effortlessly with a clean and intuitive interface.



#### 7. Product Principles
These principles will guide every future decision.
- Principle 1 — User Decision First
Every feature should help users discover, understand, or decide what to watch.
- Principle 2 — Simplicity Over Complexity
Present information clearly.
Avoid unnecessary clutter.
- Principle 3 — Fast by Design
Users should get meaningful content quickly.
Performance is a product feature.
- Principle 4 — Reliable Experience
The application should remain useful even when network conditions are poor.
(This principle will later justify our Offline-First architecture.)
- Principle 5 — Purpose-Driven Engineering
Technology choices should support product goals—not the other way around.
We will select tools and architectures based on long-term maintainability, scalability, and user experience.


#### 8. Success Metrics (MVP)
We should define how we'll know if we've achieved our vision.

Examples:
- A user can find a movie within a few interactions.
- Movie details load quickly and are easy to scan.
- Users can save movies for later viewing.
- Navigation feels simple and predictable.
- The app remains usable with cached content when offline.

#### 9. Scope
In Scope (MVP)
- Movie discovery
- Movie search
- Movie details
- Watchlist
- Offline support
- Clean navigation

Out of Scope (Future Releases)
- User accounts
- Social sharing
- Reviews written by users
- Streaming
- Payments
- AI recommendations
- Multi-platform sync
